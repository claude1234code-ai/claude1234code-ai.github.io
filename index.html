FAQ

Before you ask.

>How long does a project take?+

Most landing pages ship in 3–5 days. Full sites take 1–2 weeks. You'll know the timeline before we start — no surprises.

>How many revisions do I get?+

Two rounds are included in every price. Most projects never need a third — but if yours does, we'll figure it out together.

>How does payment work?+

Half upfront to lock in the project, half on delivery. UPI or bank transfer — whatever's easiest on your end.

>Who owns the final files?+

You do. Once it's paid for, the code and files are yours outright — no licensing, no recurring fee to me.
